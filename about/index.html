About The Still Got It Collective

Curating nights you talk about the next morning, with just the right mix of nostalgia, energy, and community.

What we do

The Still Got It Collective designs party experiences for people who still love the dance floor—but also love a reasonable bedtime. From 80’s synth nights to 90’s day parties, every event is built around great music, thoughtful details, and a crowd that actually shows up to dance.

Where we are

We’re based in Cleveland, Ohio and work with venues, DJs, and partners across the city to keep things feeling fresh while respecting the neighborhoods we’re in.

How to work with us

Interested in collaborating, sponsoring an event, or bringing the Still Got It energy to your venue? Head over to our contact page and reach out—we’d love to talk.
