What makes us different

The Still Got It Collective isn't your typical DJ night or nostalgia trip. We design complete party experiences for people who still love the dance floor—but also love a reasonable bedtime. No gimmicks, no costume requirements, no awkward ice-breakers. Just exceptional music, thoughtful production, and a room full of people who came to dance.

From 80's synth nights to 90's day parties, every event is curated around era-specific deep cuts (not just the obvious hits), professional sound and lighting, and welcoming energy that works whether you show up solo or with a crew.

Where we are

Based in Cleveland, Ohio since 2024, we partner with established local venues like B-Side Lounge to deliver consistent, high-quality events. Our relationships with professional DJs and sound engineers mean every show meets the same standard—reliable start times, quality equipment, and attention to detail.
